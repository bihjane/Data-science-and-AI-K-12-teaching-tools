## The steps every notebook follows
Load the data, explore it with a graph, prepare features, split into training and test sets, train, measure the error, improve, explain the model, then predict and check its limits. Each step has a **Your turn** task.

## How to open a notebook
- Click **Open in Colab** in the table, or
- Go to colab.research.google.com, choose **File > Open notebook > GitHub**, and paste this repository's address, or
- Download the .ipynb file and choose **File > Upload notebook** in Colab.

Then choose **File > Save a copy in Drive** so students keep their own work.

## Data sources
olympics.com (100 m winning times), United Nations (world population milestones), Gapminder via the plotly library (life expectancy and income, 142 countries), scikit-learn built-in datasets (iris flowers, handwritten digits), and optionally the MNIST dataset through TensorFlow.
